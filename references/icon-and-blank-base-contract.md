# Illustrated Asset And Blank Base Contract

Apply `manifest-eligibility-filter.md` first. Omit ordinary label text while
preserving eligible blank bases and embedded identity marks.

## Illustrated Icons And Rewards

Inventory before generation:

- outer silhouette
- attached glow, highlight, rim, or shadow
- opaque and intentionally translucent materials
- embedded emblem, mark, or identity lettering
- internal openings and color blocks
- meaningful sub-object count

Request the whole approved icon or reward in the first ImageGen result. Do not
cut it out or derive Alpha afterward.

For glass, bubbles, glow, or translucent rewards, request the solid `#00FF00`
screen in that same first result while keeping the subject material neutral.
Reject green reflected, spilled, or transmitted inside the asset.

Reject missing identity marks, altered member counts, broad material smears,
duplicated contours, or foreign UI attached to the subject.

## Icon And Label Ownership

Treat an illustrated icon and an independently variable label base as separate
proposed assets unless the user explicitly combines them. Do not create an
ordinary label-text asset.

When the user approves a combined icon-and-base delivery object, generate the
complete approved group once and record its membership.

## Blank Base Reconstruction

Request the complete owned base, not only a local text patch. Preserve:

- silhouette and corner radius
- gradient direction and color bands
- highlights, bevel, rim, and outline
- attached backplate, ornament, shadow, or glow

Exclude ordinary text and values. Reject text residue, pseudo-text, generic
replacement pills, broken gradients, repeated texture, missing bevels, or
foreign controls.

## Grouped Sets

For a user-approved grouped set, preserve exact membership and meaningful count.
Do not split members into separate outputs, omit a member, or add an unapproved
sibling.

## Background Plate

For the background asset, preserve the original scene style and composition.
Remove foreground UI and repair only exposed holes. Do not replace the scene
with a merely similar new environment.

## Output Boundary

Inspect the first generated image directly. Do not run black/white Alpha checks,
matting, segmentation, source overlays, or transparent-background tests.
